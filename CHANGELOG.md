# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- 16 built-in sound packs synthesized from scratch, plus Chaos mode.
- Song mode, Melody mode and Wobble pitch modes.
- Sustain while held: tonal packs keep sounding until the key is released, then fade out.
- Echo effect with short and long settings.
- 3D stereo: keys are panned by their position on the keyboard.
- On-screen effects (bullet holes, lasers, notes, confetti and more), a combo meter and milestone banners.
- Per-app sounds, and auto-mute while the microphone is in use or a full-screen app is open.
- Custom packs from audio files or microphone recordings, shareable as `.kbpack` files.
- Typing stats: keys per day, words per minute, streaks, a key heatmap and achievements.
- System tray menu, global mute shortcut, start with the computer, automatic updates.
